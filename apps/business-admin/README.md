# Admobitics Business Administration Portal

**Target Platform:** Flutter (Web / Desktop)  
**Tenant:** Admobitics (Commercial Advertising Operator)

## Capabilities
- Campaign creation and lifecycle management.
- Day-parting schedule matrices and screen group targeting.
- Media asset upload and pre-signed Cloudflare R2 coordination.
- Playlist composition and preview.
- Playback analytics and advertiser proof-of-play reports.
- Admobitics tenant user and role management.

*Note: Admobitics users have zero access to master licensing, device authorization, or HINOPT private keys.*
